Bug-fix release of **BetterFxSound** that makes simultaneous outputs reliable. A full review of the multiple-output engine found 91 defects behind outputs that sometimes went silent, drifted out of sync or came back at the wrong volume. This release fixes them.

> **Pre-release.** This build is for testing. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/xp3z41x/BetterFxSound/issues).

> **Run the delay sync again after upgrading.** The way delays are measured and applied changed, so delays saved by 1.0.1 or earlier are cleared on the first start.

## Fixes

### Outputs that went silent or dropped out
- Device events that have nothing to do with your outputs no longer stop and restart the audio. Examples: a monitor going to sleep, an unused DAC, a microphone changing state.
- A device change that arrived while the engine was restarting is no longer lost. Before, it could leave the sound on the wrong device until something else changed.
- When the engine fails to start, it retries on its own. When an output can't be opened, another enabled output takes over playback.
- An output that stops playing without reporting an error is noticed within 2 seconds and reopened. An output that can't be opened is retried automatically.
- Short sounds after silence are no longer held back or cut. Stops, restarts and dropouts now fade in and out instead of clicking.
- Outputs no longer stay muted after the computer wakes from sleep.
- When an HDMI or DisplayPort output disappears, the other outputs keep playing instead of going silent for 3 seconds.
- Effects and EQ work again on 3-, 5- and 7-channel devices, and on a headset that switches to mono for a call.

### Outputs drifting out of sync
- Every output, including the low-latency one, now corrects its own clock drift. Outputs that were in sync stay in sync for hours, including USB and HDMI devices.
- Outputs no longer pick up a random offset of up to 40 ms after each pause in the audio.
- Delays no longer change when a different device takes over the low-latency path.
- The low-latency output now plays at the right speed at 16, 22.05, 24, 32 and 64 kHz.
- Changing an output's delay no longer repeats or skips audio.
- Delay sync now measures after the outputs have settled, and stops cleanly on cancel, power off, sleep or a device change. A microphone that belongs to a headset being measured is refused. Outputs that couldn't be opened are reported as such.

### Volume
- Each device's own Windows volume comes back correctly in every case: after an unplug, a crash, a late or failed hand-back, or exclusive-mode use by another app. A device is never left at 100%.
- A volume change you make on an output device is now kept.
- A Windows volume or mute change made while BetterFxSound was off or closed is applied when it starts.
- Calls no longer duck BetterFxSound's own audio. When your call device is also an enabled output, it now follows the Windows volume too.
- BetterFxSound's streams no longer appear as extra entries in the Volume Mixer.

### Choosing devices
- Outputs are now tracked by device ID instead of by name. Two devices with the same name each get their own output, and a renamed device keeps its settings.
- The low-latency device is picked by your priority list after every device change. A preferred device that comes back takes that role back.
- Turning on the PC or logging in while BetterFxSound is off no longer makes a real device the Windows default.
- At logon, BetterFxSound waits up to 15 seconds for its virtual device instead of closing.
- Left, Right and Mono outputs affect only the front speakers. On multichannel devices, center and surround are mixed in.

### Stability
- The window no longer freezes when outputs are removed or devices connect, including Bluetooth devices.
- Closing the app no longer hangs on a stuck audio driver.
- Recent output changes are saved at once, so a crash no longer loses them.
- EQ and preset changes no longer cause audio glitches.
- The audio threads use Windows' pro-audio scheduling, and the PC can sleep more deeply while BetterFxSound is idle.

### Command line, status and MCP server
- `status.json` has a new `engine` section with the real state of every output: open, playing, latency, and counters for errors, dropouts and resyncs. fxmcp reads it.
- `--output`, `--enable-output`, `--disable-output`, `--output-volume` and `--output-delay` accept a device ID. **Changed:** a device name now applies to every device with that name.
- fxmcp output tools accept device IDs and report "applied" only once the change has actually taken effect.

## Installing

Download `BetterFxSound-1.0.2-x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It upgrades 1.0.0 or 1.0.1 in place and keeps your settings, presets and output setup. You only need to run the delay sync again.

## Known issues

- **The installer is not code-signed.** Windows SmartScreen will warn that it comes from an unknown publisher. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Apps set to play straight to an output's device**, instead of to FxSound, are not enhanced and play at that device's full level, ignoring the Windows volume and mute. Leave apps on the default device (FxSound).
- **The Surround effect blends the two sides.** With Surround above 0, an output set to Left or Right also plays part of the other side, quietly. Set Surround to 0 for a clean Left/Right split.
- The fork's new text is in English in every language.
- x64 only. Windows 7/8 are not supported.
- On the first start after an upgrade, the app resets its effect engine state (`HKCU\SOFTWARE\DFX`) to the selected preset, as FxSound does on every version change. Your presets and app settings are kept.
